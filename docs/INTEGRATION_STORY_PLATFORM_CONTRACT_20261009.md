## Canon-safe narrative bridge
1. Circle of Life: the gods, Nature, Aquatic, Terrain, established Legendary Eco-Kin, and ecological interdependence establish Rebearth's history.
2. Ancient conflicts: warfare, Eco-Kin exploitation, Havok/Havik history and old engineering leave battlefield ruins, damaged waterways, displaced communities, and recoverable technologies. Confirm exact character spelling and status in canon before writing dialogue.
3. Lord Dred's influence: viruses, Nanonite, pollution, and Crimson-related ecological threats compound the damage; avoid inventing a single unsupported origin for every hazard.
4. Rebuilding generations: settlements, farms, cities, transport, Aether-Ark research, and later technology arise from studied historical artifacts and local ingenuity; map established region/city IDs before new additions.
5. Arrival: humans fall from Earth through a portal onto Rebearth. They remain human, study alien Eco-Kin and gods, survive and bond through A.E.G.I.S. and Anima-Link. No human-to-Eco-Kin fusion.
6. Restoration campaign: the player establishes a personal ecological domain, constructs bases/farms/factories, purifies habitats, assists NPC communities, confronts weaponization, and ultimately opposes Lord Dred. The restored world persists into later quests.
